Methodology: Decoupling the Record from the Label

Why the "Women, Power & Personality" archive abandons pseudo-scientific percentages in favor of a tripartite marginalia architecture.

1. The Epistemological Impasse of Retrospective Diagnosis

Clinical psychiatry depends on standardized diagnostic interviews, longitudinal observation, and direct self-reporting. Retroactively assigning DSM or ICD category codes to figures from distant centuries commits a fundamental category error: anachronistically weaponizing modern clinical rubrics to explain political statecraft in eras of existential factional warfare.

2. The Weaponization of Medicine Against Female Sovereign Agency

From the classical wandering womb (*hystera*) and the Renaissance Inquisitorial demonization of female intellect to Victorian moral insanity and 20th-century tabloid hysteria, autonomous women exercising political or creative power have consistently had their actions delegitimized as mental disorder.

3. The Three Voices: Record, Label, Archive

Every file in this archive is structured around three distinct voices:

  • The Record: Documented historical events, verifiable treaties, and political actions rendered in primary serif type.
  • The Label: What rival chroniclers, ecclesiastical tribunals, or mass media called her, positioned as marginalia.
  • The Archive: Our editorial critique rendered in editor's red pencil, striking through gendered tropes and restoring historical context.

4. Strict Quotation Attribution

All quotes are taxonomically tagged: Documented (verifiable written words), Attributed (reported by ancient historians), Literary (fictional dialogue such as Frankenstein's monster), or Apocryphal (modern inventions, such as the famous quote attributed to Hypatia in 1908).
