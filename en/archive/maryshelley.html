Romantic Era England, London · 1797 AD – 1851 AD

Mary Shelley

Mary Wollstonecraft Godwin Shelley

Author of Frankenstein and master of Gothic Romantic literature; transformed profound personal bereavement and trauma into modernity's foundational cautionary myth.

«Beware; for I am fearless, and therefore powerful.»
Spoken by the Creature in Frankenstein (1818) Literary

Literary dialogue from the Creature, frequently misattributed directly to the author herself.

۰۱

What She Did (The Record)

1816 AD The Summer of 1816 at Villa Diodati; conceives and drafts Frankenstein at age eighteen.
1822 AD Percy Bysshe Shelley drowns; Mary returns to London to raise her surviving son and edit Percy's literary canon.
«Melancholic Gothic Morbidist»
Victorian literary critics · 19th Century

Exploration of resurrection, monstrosity, and mortality in Frankenstein.

✎ Archive Reading

Victorian society pathologized a young woman's dark philosophic imagination rather than acknowledging scientific insight.

Personality Reading (Non-Diagnostic)

Methodological Note: This is not a clinical diagnosis. Retrospective diagnosis without clinical interview is epistemologically impossible. What follows is a non-pathologizing synthesis of documented history.

Mary Shelley turned devastating bereavement into enduring philosophical art. Frankenstein remains a monumental critique of irresponsible scientific hubris and societal cruelty.
