Kingdom of France, Florence & Paris · 1519 AD – 1589 AD

Catherine de' Medici

Caterina Maria Romula di Lorenzo de' Medici

Queen mother and regent of France; an Italian diplomat who fought to preserve the Valois monarchy amid the brutal French Wars of Religion.

«I have done everything in human power to prevent my country from tearing itself apart.»
Letters of Catherine de' Medici Documented

Documented in primary letters preserved in the French National Archives.

Personality Reading (Non-Diagnostic)

Methodological Note: This is not a clinical diagnosis. Retrospective diagnosis without clinical interview is epistemologically impossible. What follows is a non-pathologizing synthesis of documented history.

Catherine de' Medici was caught between militant Catholic Guises and rebellious Huguenots. Modern revisionist scholarship recognizes her as a pragmatic conciliation diplomat rather than an evil mastermind.
