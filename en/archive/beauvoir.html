Existentialist France, Paris · 1908 AD – 1986 AD

Simone de Beauvoir

Simone Lucie Ernestine Marie Bertrand de Beauvoir

Existentialist philosopher, public intellectual, and feminist theorist; author of The Second Sex, unmasking the cultural machinery of gendered subjugation.

«One is not born, but rather becomes, a woman.»
The Second Sex (1949), Vol. II Documented

Documented foundational thesis separating biological sex from socially constructed gender.

۰۱

What She Did (The Record)

1949 AD Publishes The Second Sex; sparks fierce public outcry and condemnation by the Vatican Index.
1971 AD Drafts Manifesto of the 343, championing bodily and legal autonomy.
«Degenerate & Morally Corrosive»
Vatican Index of Forbidden Books and conservative press · 1950s

Philosophical deconstruction of mandatory domesticity and bourgeois marriage.

✎ Archive Reading

Beauvoir pioneered the very philosophical insight that non-conforming women are pathologized as abnormal.
