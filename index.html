      EPISTEMOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK

      Why "Women, Power & Personality"?

      Retrospective psychiatric diagnosis (assigning clinical DSM/ICD personality disorders to figures who lived centuries ago) faces severe methodological limitations due to the absence of direct clinical interviews and reliable baseline evidence.

      Furthermore, the history of psychiatry—most notably the construct of "Hysteria" and moral insanity in the 19th century—demonstrates that medical labels were frequently weaponized against women who exhibited political ambition, sexual autonomy, or intellectual defiance.

      In this research archive, we separate documented historical actions (alliances, statecraft, ruthlessness, trauma) from retrospective psychological interpretations, using modern dimensional personality theory rather than stigmatizing reductionism.
